Bonedle

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Bonedle: Domino Logic Puzzle (“Bonedle”, “the app”) is a puzzle game for Android. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and every board is generated on your phone. This policy explains the little data the app uses.

Stored on your phone

Your progress (finished and unfinished boards, times, hints used, streaks), your settings and any friend’s time from a challenge link you opened are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Android may include them in your device backup if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Analytics

To learn which features work, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record anonymous usage events, for example that a tutorial step was finished, a board was started or solved (with its difficulty, time and number of hints), a hint was used, a result was shared and which app it was shared to, or a challenge link was opened. Along with these events Firebase collects an app-instance identifier, the device model, Android version, app version, language and an approximate region derived from your IP address.

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or the content of anything you share.
  • The app does not collect the Android advertising ID for analytics.
  • Analytics data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms and kept no longer than our project’s retention setting, at most 14 months.

Challenge links and installs

When a friend sends you a Bonedle link, the link names the board and may include your friend’s time and number of hints. If you install the app from that link’s page, Google Play passes the same board details to the app (the “install referrer”) so it can open that board on first launch. The referrer contains no personal information.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one local notification a day on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server to do this, and you can turn it off in the app’s Settings or in Android settings at any time.

Sharing

When you share a result, the app creates an image of your result card and passes it, with a short text, to the app you choose in Android’s share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app’s privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any website, GitHub and Google receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads; see the GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Children

Bonedle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Reset the analytics identifier and your on-device data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • Turn notifications off at any time.
  • Ask us about this policy or your data by opening an issue at github.com/rizqtekv/bonedle/issues.

Changes

If the app starts using data in a new way, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
